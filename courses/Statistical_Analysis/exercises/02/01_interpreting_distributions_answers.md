# Interpreting Distributions — Answer Key

## Exercise 1 — Comparative Sex IQ Distribution

1. **C** — Both curves peak at the dashed line marked μ = 100; centers align even though spread differs.
2. **B** — Men’s σ ≈ 16.2 yields a wider, flatter blue curve; women’s σ ≈ 13.2 is narrower and taller.
3. **C** — For probability densities, a narrower distribution must have a higher peak so total area remains 1.
4. **B** — The wider (men’s) distribution places more density in both low and high tails away from 100.
5. **A** — If men had a higher average IQ than women, the blue (men) curve would shift right so that its center (peak) is located to the right of the red (women) curve’s center.

13. **C** — The bars are tallest in the evening, peaking around 9 p.m. — that is the mode (busiest hour).
14. **C** — Visits rise gradually from the morning (long, gentle left tail) and concentrate near the evening peak, then drop more steeply afterward: a left-skewed, unimodal shape (no distinct second lunch peak).
15. **B** — Most of the density sits in the late afternoon–evening (~6 p.m.–midnight); the early-morning hours have very short bars.
16. **B** — The shortest bars are in the morning (~6–9 a.m.), so that is the quietest, least crowded time to visit.
