# Syntopical Reading Assistant

## Overview

Single-book reading has an inherent limitation: you see the subject through one author's lens.

[**Syntopical reading**](https://chapterly.ai/blog/syntopical-reading-guide) goes beyond any single book. You read multiple works on the same subject, compare and contrast their approaches, and construct your own understanding of the topic that is not dependent on any one author's perspective.

Read about [Synpotical Reading](https://chapterly.ai/blog/syntopical-reading-guide) which specifies 5 key steps:

1. Find the Relevant Passages
2. Establish a Shared Vocabulary
3. Define the Key Questions
4. Analyze the Discussions
5. Formulate Your Own Analysis

The idea could be generalized to: [Comparative Analysis](https://genedwrites.fas.harvard.edu/tfs-tas/comparative-analysis).

## Bonus

Voice interface; speak, hear, and interrupt. Maintain low latency (near real-time).
