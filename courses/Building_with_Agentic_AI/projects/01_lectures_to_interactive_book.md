# Deep Transcriber: from Lectures to a Book

## Overview

**Goal:** make the material of recorded lectures interactive.

**Phase 1:** transcribe a sequence of long-form YouTube video/audio lectures (15-50 minutes or longer) into a sequence of articles that read like a complete book. 

Two versions

1. written in Arabic (مع تشكيل تام)
2. written in the original language (if not already Arabic)

**Phase 2:** allow the learner to interact with it to learn better.

The user may ask:

- "Was concept X introduced before lecture 4? and what does it say? and where can I find it?"
- "What does the author mean by saying: so and so? isn't that conflicting with them saying: so and so?"
- "I read somewhere else that concept X is so and so; is my understanding correct according to this?"
- "I want the full definition of X (from wikipedia) followed by a complete accurate translation of that in Arabic, and examples."
- "Please provide more examples on concept X in layman terms using analogy to cars."
- "Explain X assuming I know nothing about Y at all. Relate this to me as a software developer."
- "What does the math say? break it term-by-term then, let's try to read it like plain English, in layman terms; keep it simple for me.".

Test this on any open eductaion lectures already available on **YouTube**.

## Evaluation and Optimization

Come up with a rubric to evaluate and optimize your system.

Manual review will be necessary to make sure sections weren't dropped from the final output, so pick something you already have gone through entirely before so that it's easy to revise and improve your pipeline.

## Extension

Scale this to accept a list of playlists; and do batch processing.

Scale this to accept inputs from other platforms (not just YouTube).
