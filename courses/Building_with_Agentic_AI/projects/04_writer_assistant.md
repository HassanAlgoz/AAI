# Writer Assistant

## Overview

Goal: assist writers in throuought their writing process to produce a strong piece of long-form content (a book); given any topic of interest.

Non-goal: to have the agent do the writing end-to-end.

## The 10-Step Writing Process

[Peterson](https://jordanbpeterson.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/02/Essay_Writing_Guide.docx) recommends a structured approach to ensure quality and depth:

1. **Define a Central Question:** Identify a single, meaningful problem you want to solve.
2. **Develop Sub-questions:** Create roughly 10 sub-questions that branch from the central inquiry.
3. **Outline:** This is mandatory. An essay is a "skeleton" of ideas that must be organized logically.
4. **Reading and Research:** Compile a reading list (aim for 5–10 sources per 1,000 words). Take two to three times more notes than you think you need, rewriting them in your own voice.
5. **Drafting:** Don't aim for perfection. Focus on getting the ideas down. Aim for a length slightly longer than your target to allow for aggressive trimming later.
6. **Production vs. Editing:** Separate the acts of writing and editing. Once the draft exists, list sentences individually and refine them.
7. **Refine Sentences:** Strive for brevity. Aim to cut 15–25% of word count by eliminating unnecessary words. Use the exact word for the exact meaning; avoid "camouflage" or overly complex vocabulary.
8. **Reorder Paragraphs:** Once sentences are sharpened, re-evaluate the flow of your paragraphs to ensure a logical progression.
9. **Generate New Outline:** After editing, try to reconstruct your argument from memory to see if the structure holds.
10. **References:** Ensure all sources are properly cited.

## Checks

Assistance includes quick checks and verifications that would usually require lots of time from the writer. For example:

- Checking facts: were A and B contemporary?
- Did I cover everything in resources R1, R2, and R3 about topic X?
- Did I represent the argument X from the point of view of R1 accurately?
- Check all the definitions I have and point out if I have deviated from these sources: R1 and R2
- ..etc.

## Evaluation and Optimization

Come up with a rubric to score any piece of long-form writing.

Example (for technical writing):

- intuition of the idea before the formal definition
- formal definition must be followed by examples
- each term in the definition must either have been explained before, or should be explained right after the examples of the definition. 
- ...etc.

Construct metrics from this rubric to optimize your writer AI.
